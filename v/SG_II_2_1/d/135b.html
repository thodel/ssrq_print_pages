53This is a transcription of a historical legal document from Rapperswil, dated January 29, 1555. The document discusses the establishment of a guild or society dedicated to Saint Erhard. It outlines the rights, duties, and financial obligations of the members. The text is written in an older German orthography, with some archaic spellings and abbreviations. The document is part of the "Sammlung Schweizerischer Rechtsquellen" (Collection of Swiss Legal Sources). The transcription aims to preserve the original text as accurately as possible, including line breaks and hyphenation. The document is a primary source for understanding the legal and social structures of the time. The use of "ß" and other special characters is maintained to reflect the original text. The document is a valuable resource for historians and researchers studying Swiss legal history. The transcription is intended to be a faithful representation of the original document.
